The three body problem is a hypothetical concept in which there are two bodies of arbitrary positive mass that move in circles about their center of mass under the influence of gravity and the motion of a third body of mass zero does not affect the motion of the other two bodies. It is assumed that zero mass point moves in the plane is determined by the circles and close to one of the positive mass points, which corresponds to lunar orbits. For convenience, the gravitational constants are normalized, that is, the distance between the two positive mass-points and their sum of the masses to be one.
